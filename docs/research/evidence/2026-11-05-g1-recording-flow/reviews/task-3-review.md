#### Important (Should Fix)

1. **The upload path rehashes the entire recording twice per chunk and serializes other sessions' DB access.** `packages/web/src/lib/recording-save-flow.ts:99` iterates every chunk and calls `recheck()` before upload (`:102`) and after readback (`:116`). Each recheck calls `recorderDB.readSave` (`:56`), which executes the same full-byte validation as a write (`packages/web/src/lib/recorder-db.ts:197`). `saveTransaction` holds a readwrite transaction over the global `sessions` and `chunks` stores (`:58`) while reading and hashing every chunk (`:81`–`:91`), with repeated IDB requests keeping that transaction alive. An N-chunk recording therefore causes at least 2N complete local-byte scans during upload alone, in addition to API-boundary scans. At the accepted 2,000-chunk/100MiB limits, that is at least 400,000MiB (~391GiB) copied/hashed locally for a 100MiB draft. Concurrent captures in other sessions also queue behind these store-wide transactions. This is an algorithmic bound from code, not an elapsed-time benchmark, and should be corrected before accepting the browser path. Preserve full-byte freeze and final seal/confirmation/cleanup checks plus raw-tamper rejection; use a bounded verification approach between network steps and for each selected upload chunk. Add native hash-count scaling and late-tamper regressions, without weakening atomic cleanup or current-scope checks.
